# Flavor data source evaluation

Verified 2026-10-13 against the primary links below. Public access does not imply a permissive data license. Existing chemistry outputs are noncommercial; a commercial release needs a separate rights review of FooDB and the CoSyLab datasets.

| Source / primary evidence | Availability and contents | License verified / limits | Recommendation and integration |
|---|---|---|---|
| [CulinaryDB, CoSyLab / IIIT-Delhi](https://cosylab.iiitd.edu.in/culinarydb/) | Live landing page and downloadable four-CSV [bundle](https://cosylab.iiitd.edu.in/culinarydb/static/data/CulinaryDB.zip). Local download contains 45,772 unique recipe IDs and 26 cuisine labels; website summary says over 22 regions. Includes ingredient/entity aliases and recipe ingredient rows. | Landing page explicitly CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Attribution and share-alike apply to derivatives; noncommercial only. | **Integrated, highest value additional dataset.** `cuisine-frequencies.json` gives actual corpus proportions with denominators. Explicit part/form ambiguities are approximate; no generic pepper stand-in for named chili cultivars. Does not replace existing LLM cuisines yet. |
| [RecipeDB, CoSyLab](https://cosylab.iiitd.edu.in/recipedb/) | Live searchable repository; home page describes over 118,000 recipes, 74 countries, ingredients, processes and nutrition. Bulk export was not established in this verification. | Site explicitly CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. | Documented only. More detailed cuisine geography could improve coverage, but CulinaryDB's downloadable tables are easier to reproduce. Avoid scraping individual recipes when a licensed bulk resource suffices. |
| [Pyrfume](https://pyrfume.org/) / [public archive](https://github.com/pyrfume/pyrfume-data) / [authors' data paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557823/) | Live site/repository; molecule CIDs, structures, odor descriptors and psychophysical datasets. Archive includes Flavornet, FooDB and commercial-source collections. | Repository advertises MIT, but that is insufficient evidence that all underlying third-party data have MIT redistribution rights. Dataset-specific provenance and upstream permissions must be checked. Paper license is not a blanket archive license. | Documented only. Strong future option for perceptual validation after choosing a specifically licensed human-rating dataset. Do not indiscriminately ingest the aggregate archive. |
| [Flavornet, Acree & Arn / DATU](https://flavornet.org/) | Live; 738 odorants listed, GC–olfactometry evidence, sensory descriptors, retention indices and bibliographies. Site distinguishes estimates from experimental measurements. | No explicit open data redistribution license found on the primary landing page. Public viewing is not permission to redistribute. | Documented only; ask permission before integration. Valuable for odor-active molecule filtering, but molecule descriptors alone do not establish ingredient concentrations. |
| [Ahn et al. 2011, Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep00196) | Live article with two supplementary ZIP links for ingredient/compound and recipe data. Browser detected binary ZIP responses but did not parse them; no new download ingested. | Article explicitly CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; preserve attribution and examine supplement-specific notices before redistribution. | Documented only. Useful replication baseline, largely overlaps the existing chemistry/recipe layers. No Ahn dataset is currently used. |
| [RecipeNLG authors' site](https://recipenlg.cs.put.poznan.pl/dataset) / [paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.inlg-1.4/) / [authors' repository](https://github.com/Glorf/recipenlg) | Site responds but browser extracts no text. Paper and scripts available; over two million recipe records, ingredient text and NER entities; combines gathered recipes with Recipe1M+ records. | Could not verify a current dataset redistribution license from the primary dataset page in this session. Script availability does not license underlying recipes. | Documented only. Do not integrate until downloadable data terms and upstream rights are confirmed. Large co-occurrence corpus, but cuisine attribution and source heterogeneity need validation. |
| [Recipe1M+, MIT authors' page](https://pic2recipe.csail.mit.edu/) | Live paper/model/code page. Recipe text and food images support co-occurrence research; direct current dataset download/acceptance terms not established on the current page. | A current primary dataset license was not verified here; do not substitute a code license for data terms or assume commercial reuse. | Documented only. No integration; prefer CulinaryDB's explicit license and cuisine labels. |
| [Wikidata licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing) | Live structured-data service: botanical names, taxon identifiers, origin statements and references; individual completeness varies. | Structured main/Property/Lexeme/EntitySchema data **CC0**; other page text CC BY-SA 4.0. | Documented only. Good next metadata source; distinguish plant origin, culinary adoption and product processing origin. Not an aroma or cuisine-frequency dataset. |
| [NMSU heat methods](https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_h/H237/index.html), [cultivar trials](https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_circulars/CR706/index.html), [extension heat chart](https://bernalilloextension.nmsu.edu/documents/redchile-part3.pdf) | Live extension resources. Scoville/HPLC methods and measured cultivar values. Cayenne-type cultivar values vary substantially; commercial powders are not a single cultivar. | H237 permits educational reproduction and reserves other rights. We cite facts and methods; no tables, images or datasets copied. | Reference only, not a second dataset integration. Do not turn pure-molecule pungency or cultivar numbers into purported measurements of arbitrary spice products. |
| [Jordt et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02282), [ITC activity study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26263397/), [pungency overview](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10296967/) | Primary receptor study and ITC comparison establish mustard/horseradish/wasabi mechanisms; overview discusses pepper and ginger compounds. | Papers have individual publisher terms; referenced findings only, no imported dataset or copied figures. | Evidence references for pungency. A universal numeric rank across pepper, ginger and mustard preparations is not justified by these studies. Mustard species, processing, hydration and serving dose matter. |

## Attribution and outputs

`src/data/aroma-fingerprints.json` and `docs/analysis/aroma-method.json` derive from FlavorDB2 (Garg et al., NAR 2018, [doi:10.1093/nar/gkx957](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkx957), CoSyLab; **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0**) and FooDB compound flavor annotations ([terms](https://foodb.ca/), noncommercial; commercial permission required). They retain source/entity quality. This is a transformation into a fixed vocabulary and IDF-weighted normalized vectors; distribute this derivative under [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/) subject also to FooDB's terms. No USDA descriptors are invented: USDA has no descriptor annotations in the local extract.

`src/data/cuisine-frequencies.json` credits **CulinaryDB, Complex Systems Laboratory, IIIT-Delhi, Ganesh Bagler**, links its download and CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 license, records retrieval date and SHA-256 for all four source CSVs, and explains the transformation. This derivative is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Raw recipe text is not shipped. `python3 ingestion/ingest_culinarydb.py --download` rebuilds the ignored raw cache; subsequent runs are offline. Only CulinaryDB was added as a new bulk source.

Existing FlavorGraph outputs remain Apache-2.0 (Park et al. 2021); FooDB/USDA/FlavorDB2 overlap retains its existing attribution and licensing. Gemini profiles and cuisine claims remain generated estimates, with compound context supplied where available.
